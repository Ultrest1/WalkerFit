Privacy Policy

WalkerFit Tracker
Last updated: March 11, 2026

WalkerFit Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use WalkerFit Tracker on iPhone.

Information We Collect

WalkerFit Tracker may process the following information to provide app functionality:

  • Location data, to record and display your walking, running, or cycling routes.
  • Purchase and subscription information, to manage premium access and restore purchases.
  • User identifiers generated by subscription or purchase services, to verify entitlements.

How Information Is Used

Your information is used only to operate core app features, including:

  • Tracking workouts and route history inside the app.
  • Showing your route on the map during and after an activity.
  • Managing subscriptions, premium access, and purchase restoration.

Location Data

WalkerFit Tracker requests location access so the app can record routes and provide live activity tracking. If you deny location access, route recording features may not work properly.

Subscriptions and Third-Party Services

WalkerFit Tracker uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchase status. RevenueCat may process purchase-related data and app user identifiers on our behalf to support subscription functionality.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is only shared with service providers when needed to operate the app, such as subscription infrastructure.

Data Retention

Data stored on your device remains there unless you remove the app or delete the data. Purchase-related records may be retained by Apple or our subscription service provider as required to support billing and restoration.

Your Choices

  • You can disable location access at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • You can manage subscriptions through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children's Privacy

WalkerFit Tracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
